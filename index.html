The opening

Traveler,

This letter did not travel by honest post. It found the cracks in your door, the pause in your sleep, the moment you were already listening.

You are expected at a house that has been waiting longer than any of its graves.

The approach

Welcome to the house that watches

Dead trees bow toward the gate. The path remembers every guest who came this way and did not leave the same.

A single window burns with a color that is not fire. Something in the cellar turns over in its sleep.

The threshold

The door yields without a key

Dust hangs like breath. Candle smoke writes letters that vanish when you look at them directly. Footprints mark the stairs that were not there a moment ago.

Vanquish the undead

This is not a metaphor the house will forgive. It is a gathering, a hunt, and a birthday written in bone.
